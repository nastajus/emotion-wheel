    About translation

    English is the reference vocabulary. Emotion words do not always map one-to-one across languages, so some labels are close interpretations rather than exact equivalents.

    Definition origin

    English definitions were AI-drafted for this project. Non-English versions are machine-translated from those drafts. They are bundled locally and have not been professionally reviewed.

    Navigation controls

    Open a word
    Click or tap a word. Release without dragging.
    Pan the wheel
    Drag anywhere across the wheel.
    Zoom
    Scroll over the wheel. Zoom stays centered beneath the pointer.
    Explore a ring
    Scroll over an open word popup, or hold Shift and scroll anywhere over the wheel.
    Cycle languages
    Hover over the language selector and scroll. Only checked languages are included, and the list loops.
    Toggle sidebar
    Press Ctrl + Shift + Alt + S.
    Close
    Press Esc to close popups and menus.
